# SustainMetric AI Audit Results

The following are the documented AI audit results from testing the `sustainmetric-idx` FastMCP server's `trigger_green_audit` and `get_audit_status` tools using real Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) emiten data (PGEO, ADRO, BREN, and BUMI).

*(Disclaimer: Information & Analysis Tool Only. Not Financial Advice or Investment Recommendation.)*

### 1. PGEO (Pertamina Geothermal Energy Tbk)
* **Quadrant:** Transisi Tangguh (Q1)
* **Definition:** High green alignment backed by robust cash flow and Capex. True sustainable compounder.
* **TKBI Alignment:** **Hijau** (Electricity Generation from Geothermal Energy)
* **Consistency Score:** 100.0
* **Viability Score:** 92.0
* **Audit Findings:**
  * Core activity aligns with TKBI 2024 'Hijau' criteria (Electricity Generation from Geothermal Energy).
  * Strong capital commitment: Capex/Revenue ratio is 35.5%.
  * Documented clean energy deployment backed by verified disclosures.

### 2. ADRO (Adaro Energy Indonesia Tbk)
* **Quadrant:** Sumber Kas Konvensional (Q3)
* **Definition:** High cash generation with legacy/fossil profile and low TKBI alignment. High greenwashing vulnerability if claiming green status.
* **TKBI Alignment:** **Transisi** (Coal-Fired Power Plant Early Retirement & Managed Phase-Out)
* **Consistency Score:** 15.0
* **Viability Score:** 100.0
* **Audit Findings:**

  * Capital expenditure is primarily targeted at maintaining legacy operations (10.0% of revenue).
  * Disclosures align with conventional legacy energy operations.
